Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?

You can permanently delete your account at any time. In the app, navigate to Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account. This permanently removes all your posts, workout logs, nutritional metrics, and personal data from our servers.

How do I report inappropriate content or harassment?

Tap the three dots (…) on any post, comment, or user profile and select Report. Our safety moderation team reviews all reports within 24 hours in accordance with our Community Guidelines.

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? to receive a secure one-time verification code (OTP) sent to your registered email address.

How do I make my account private?

In the app, navigate to Settings > Privacy > Private Account. When enabled, only users whom you have manually approved as followers can see your posts, workouts, and profile activity.

How is my location data handled?

Your device location is only accessed when you explicitly start an Outdoor Run or Ride session. We do not track your location in the background when the workout tracker is disabled.
